### Put AI to work across your business.

Bandpey is an agentic AI engineering company in Munich. Our engineers join your team
and build AI agents, connected workflows and custom software inside your environment,
from business workflow to bare metal. We build our own products on the same foundations.
[bandpey.com](https://bandpey.com)

### Products

- **[FACTHORY](https://github.com/FACTHORYAI)** · [facthory.com](https://facthory.com)
  Enterprise operational intelligence: a living model of your people, processes,
  systems and knowledge that AI agents research, reason and act on. As a managed cloud
  service, in your own cloud, or fully on premises.
- **[Emolysis](https://github.com/emolysis)** · [emolysis.com](https://emolysis.com)
  The agentic lakehouse for data engineering and analytics. An entire data team. One
  download. In private development; early access by invitation.
- **[Inteliwert](https://github.com/inteliwertai)**
  Find relevant tenders and grants. Get to the evidence faster: German and EU
  opportunities and the documents behind them, searchable in one place. In development.

### How we build

- **Useful over theatrical.** Systems that solve a real operational problem, not demos
  that can't carry the work.
- **Trust is a product feature.** Provenance, permissions, evaluation and human review
  are part of the build.
- **Built to compound.** Every engagement leaves stronger software, sharper practices
  and a better platform for the next one.
